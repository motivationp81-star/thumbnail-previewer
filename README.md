# 👀 Thumbnail Previewer

**See how your YouTube thumbnail really looks — before you publish.**

Upload a thumbnail and instantly preview it the way viewers will see it:

- 🏠 **Home page (desktop)** — your video in a grid, next to other videos
- 🔍 **Search results** — the wide list layout
- 📱 **Mobile** — inside a phone frame, in the scroll feed
- 🔬 **Tiny-size test** — your thumbnail shrunk to sidebar size, so you can check it is still readable

You can edit the video title, channel name, duration badge and the views/time line to match your real upload.

## Why it helps

Most thumbnails are designed full-screen, but viewers meet them *small* — in a busy grid,
on a phone, next to five other videos fighting for the same click. A design that looks
great at 1280 × 720 can turn into a blur at feed size. This tool shows you the truth in
five seconds, while you can still fix it.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Your image is never uploaded anywhere.

## Tech

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no build step, no dependencies. Open `index.html` or use the live demo on GitHub Pages.
